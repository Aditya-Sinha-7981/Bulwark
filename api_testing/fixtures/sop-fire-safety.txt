Standard Operating Procedure: Fire Extinguisher Inspection

Document ID: SOP-FS-014
Category: Safety
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1. Purpose
This procedure defines the required inspection frequency and process for
portable fire extinguishers across all facility zones.

2. Inspection Frequency
- Visual inspection: monthly, by designated zone wardens.
- Full maintenance inspection: annually, by a certified technician.
- Hydrostatic testing: every 5 years for stored-pressure units, every 12
  years for units requiring recharge testing under IS 2190.

3. Visual Inspection Checklist
- Extinguisher is in its designated location and unobstructed.
- Pressure gauge reads in the operable (green) range.
- Safety pin and tamper seal are intact.
- No visible corrosion, leakage, or physical damage to the shell.
- Inspection tag is present and signed with the current date.

4. Escalation
Any extinguisher failing a visual inspection must be tagged
"OUT OF SERVICE" immediately and replaced with a serviced unit within
24 hours. Failures must be logged in the facility Safety Incident Register.

5. Responsible Roles
- Zone Warden: monthly visual inspection.
- Facilities Manager: scheduling annual certified inspection.
- Safety Officer: hydrostatic testing compliance tracking.
