Standard Operating Procedure: Rotating Equipment Preventive Maintenance

Document ID: SOP-EM-027
Category: Maintenance
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1. Purpose
Defines preventive maintenance intervals and inspection criteria for
rotating equipment (pumps, motors, compressors) in the production area.

2. Maintenance Intervals
- Lubrication check: weekly.
- Vibration analysis: monthly, using handheld vibration meter.
- Bearing temperature check: weekly, via infrared thermometer; flag any
  reading above 80 degrees Celsius for immediate investigation.
- Full teardown inspection: every 18 months or 10,000 operating hours,
  whichever comes first.

3. Acceptance Criteria
- Vibration readings must remain below 4.5 mm/s RMS (ISO 10816 Zone B).
- No audible bearing noise, grinding, or irregular pulsing.
- Lubricant level within the sight-glass operating band; no visible
  contamination or discoloration.

4. Out-of-Tolerance Response
Any reading outside acceptance criteria triggers a Work Order within
4 hours and equipment is de-rated or shut down if vibration exceeds
7.1 mm/s RMS (ISO 10816 Zone D) pending investigation.

5. Responsible Roles
- Maintenance Technician: weekly checks, work order initiation.
- Reliability Engineer: monthly vibration analysis review.
- Plant Manager: approval for equipment shutdown/de-rating decisions.
